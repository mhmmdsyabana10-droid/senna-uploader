Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Senna Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app uses Google OAuth to obtain permission to upload videos and manage video metadata (title, description, thumbnail) on the owner's YouTube channels. It does not read, collect or store data about other users.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's private server and are used only to upload the owner's own videos. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
